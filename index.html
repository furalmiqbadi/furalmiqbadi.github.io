About

How I work

I work mostly on the frontend: translating a design into Blade templates or Flutter widgets, wiring the form states, and chasing the edge cases that only show up after the happy path works. On the lab profile project I also took the controller and model work that the pages needed.

With Sign.L I took on the project manager role alongside the frontend and the model. That mostly meant splitting work by folder, keeping the architecture consistent, and making sure the camera and inference flow held up on mid-range Android devices.

I am looking for a frontend or full-stack internship where I can work inside an existing codebase and get my code reviewed by people more experienced than me.
